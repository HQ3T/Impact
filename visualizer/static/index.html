🔍 Permutation Feature Importance & Economic Mechanics

Explainable AI

Identifies which financial indicators possess the highest predictive sensitivity for corporate environmental performance.

1. Financial Slack & Cash Flow Margin

Firms with surplus operating cash flow can finance multi-year decarbonization, renewable energy PPAs, and supply chain emissions tracking without compromising solvency.

2. Capital Intensity & Asset Heaviness

Companies with vast physical plants, factories, and utility infrastructure face structural environmental compliance obligations that heavily anchor baseline scores.

3. Solvency & Debt-to-Assets

Elevated financial leverage restricts discretionary capital expenditure. Highly indebted companies prioritize debt service over voluntary environmental stewardship.

4. Industry-Relative Z-Scores

Normalizing financial health within peer groups enables the model to distinguish clean outliers in naturally carbon-heavy sectors (e.g. NextEra Energy in Utilities).
